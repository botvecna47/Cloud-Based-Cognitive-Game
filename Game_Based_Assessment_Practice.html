Game-Based Cognitive Assessment — Practice Arena

Six practice games modelled on the Capgemini game round. Each game runs 6 minutes, gets harder each level, and rewards speed. Level reward = level² ÷ time on the level.
Practice tool for training only. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Capgemini. Game mechanics are approximations built to rehearse the real format.
